My Engineering Responsibility in Team SWOT

Within Team SWOT, I was specifically responsible for the object detection, the gripping point detection (using 3D Computer Vision and the Point Cloud Library / PCL), and the autonomous gripping itself with the UR3e robotic manipulator arm.

About RoboCup@Work

The RoboCup@Work league is a premier international benchmark competition in autonomous robotics designed to accelerate applied research for Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing. Mobile robots must autonomously navigate factory floors, locate industrial workpieces, compute grasping points, and accurately place components onto dynamic fixtures.

The Challenge: Basic Manipulation Test (BMT)

In RoboCup@Work, autonomous robots must execute dynamic mission orders communicated by a central referee box. As the newly founded team SWOT at FHWS (University of Applied Sciences Würzburg-Schweinfurt), our primary mission was mastering the Basic Manipulation Test (BMT).

The BMT validates core robotic capabilities: autonomous navigation, 3D object detection, gripping point calculation, and precision component placement on varied industrial surfaces (wood, acrylic glass, turntables, shelving racks, and decoy clutter).

Hardware Platform

Our mobile manipulator integrates industrial-grade embedded hardware:

  • Omnidirectional Mobile Base: Custom chassis by Evocortex GmbH equipped with Mecanum wheels.
  • LiDAR Localization: Dual Sick TiM561 2D LiDAR scanners mounted diagonally for 360° SLAM.
  • Robotic Arm & Gripper: Universal Robots UR3e collaborative arm with a Robotiq Hand-E parallel gripper.
  • 3D Eye-in-Hand Vision: Intel RealSense D435i depth camera mounted on the gripper flange for point cloud capture.

Results & Master's Thesis

As a newcomer team at the RoboCup@Work 2021 World Championship, Team SWOT achieved an outstanding 4th place worldwide. The 3D vision object detection and gripping algorithms developed for this competition also served as the foundation of my Master of Engineering thesis at FHWS (graded 1.3).
