A Lifelong Passion for Rhythm & Movement

Balancing intense software engineering and mathematical problem-solving with the physical, dynamic realm of partner dancing has been one of my most rewarding pursuits. Partner dancing is an intuitive real-time communication protocol between two dancers, driven by musicality, elasticity, and spatial awareness.

The Diverse Dance Spectrum

Standard & Latin Ballroom

Mastering posture, swing-and-sway mechanics (Waltz, Quickstep, Tango), and rhythmic Latin hip action (Cha-Cha, Rumba, Jive).

Salsa & Bachata

Energetic social floor staples: Cuban & Cross-body Salsa turns and smooth Dominican/Sensual Bachata body isolations.

Kizomba

Deep grounded connection originating from Angola, focusing on subtle torso lead, smooth weight transfers, and intimate connection.

West Coast Swing (WCS)

A linear, slotted dance characterized by elastic tension/compression, dynamic lead-follow improvisation, and versatility across Pop, Blues, and RnB.

WSDC Competition & The BaroqueSwing Milestone

In the global West Coast Swing community, dancers compete under the official sanctioned guidelines of the World Swing Dance Council (WSDC) in randomized Jack & Jill competitions—where leaders and followers are randomly paired and dance to unannounced music.

Parallels Between Dancing & Software Engineering

Much like architecting distributed systems or mobile manipulation pipelines in robotics, partner dancing relies on precise control loops:

  • Low-Latency Feedback: Continuous micro-adjustments through physical connection and visual cues.
  • Deterministic Frameworks with Dynamic Execution: Adhering to timing and slot rules while improvising freely to musical accents.
  • Empathy & Team Synergy: Enabling both partners to express their creativity seamlessly on the floor.
