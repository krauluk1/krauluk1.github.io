About RoboCup@Work

The RoboCup@Work league is a premier international benchmark competition in autonomous robotics designed to accelerate applied research for Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing. University and research teams from across the globe compete to solve complex logistics, perception, grasping, and mobile manipulation tasks under real-world factory constraints.

The Challenge: Basic Manipulation Test (BMT)

In RoboCup@Work, autonomous robots must execute autonomous mission orders communicated by a central referee box. As the newly founded team SWOT at FHWS (University of Applied Sciences Würzburg-Schweinfurt), our primary mission was mastering the Basic Manipulation Test (BMT).

The BMT rigorously validates four essential robotic capabilities: autonomous navigation, 3D object recognition, 6-DoF grasp planning, and precision component placement and rotation on varied industrial surfaces (wood, acrylic glass, turntables, shelving racks, and decoy clutter).

Hardware Architecture: Team SWOT Platform

Our mobile manipulator integrates industrial-grade embedded hardware:

  • Omnidirectional Mobile Base: Custom chassis by Evocortex GmbH featuring Mecanum wheels, enabling holonomic sideways and diagonal translation in constrained factory lanes.
  • LiDAR Safety & Localization: Dual Sick TiM561 2D LiDAR scanners mounted diagonally to provide 360° environmental awareness for SLAM.
  • 6-DoF Robotic Arm & End-Effector: Universal Robots UR3e collaborative arm with a Robotiq Hand-E precision parallel gripper, achieving a 50 cm payload reach.
  • 3D Eye-in-Hand Vision: Intel RealSense D435i depth camera mounted directly on the gripper flange for point cloud capture.
  • Distributed Computing Cluster: Intel UP Xtreme host, NVIDIA Jetson TX2 for deep learning / vision acceleration, and an onboard Lenovo ThinkPad.

Software Architecture: Modular ROS Pipeline

The software architecture was developed in C++ and Python on Ubuntu Linux using the Robot Operating System (ROS Melodic):

  • Task Planning & Scheduler: Parses dynamic JSON/XML match referee orders, generates dependency graphs, and dispatches execution actions.
  • Autonomous SLAM Navigation: Adaptive Monte-Carlo Localization (AMCL) combined with the Timed Elastic Band (TEB) local planner for collision-free real-time trajectory optimization.
  • 3D Perception & Pose Estimation: Point Cloud Library (PCL) pipelines perform planar segmentation, cluster extraction, and 3D surface model matching to determine 6-DoF grasping coordinates.
  • Cartesian Manipulation Control: Implemented cartesian_ros_control for compliant trajectory execution and collision handling.

Worldwide Results & Master's Thesis

As a newcomer team at the RoboCup@Work 2021 World Championship, Team SWOT achieved an outstanding 4th place worldwide. The 3D vision algorithms and mobile manipulation software developed during this campaign also served as the foundation of my Master of Engineering thesis at FHWS, which was graded with highest honors (1.3).
